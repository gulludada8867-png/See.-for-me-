💡 The Problem

Visually impaired people often struggle to understand their surroundings, read text, and identify objects — especially when assistance is not readily available.

🎯 Our Solution

SeeForMe – an AI assistant that captures the world through your phone camera, understands it and helps you via voice feedback.

✦ Key Features

  • 📷 Object & scene recognition
  • 🔤 Text reading (signs, documents, labels)
  • 🔊 Natural voice responses
  • 💬 Follow-up questions on the same image
  • 🎤 Easy interaction — voice or text

Demo Scenarios

1 · Object identification

Capture a table and ask “What objects are placed in front of me?” The app identifies and describes them aloud.

2 · Text reading

Capture a signboard and ask “Can you read this sign for me?” The text is extracted and read out.

3 · Follow-up

Ask “What does the sign say about the entrance?” — answered from the same image context.
